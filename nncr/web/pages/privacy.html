NNC Reporter

How your data is used

NNC Reporter helps invited members report street issues to North Northamptonshire Council. It is run by the person or group who invited you; ask them if you have any questions about your data.

What is kept

  • Your account: email, name, role, a one-way hash of your password, and a record of sign-ins (time, IP address and browser) for security.
  • Your details for the council's forms: the name, email, phone number and address you enter. They are only sent to the council, on the forms you submit.
  • Your reports: photos, their location, and the description. Photos can show people, vehicles or homes - crop anything you do not need before submitting.
  • A highways site password, if you save one: stored encrypted, used only to sign you in to that site, never shown again.

Who sees what

  • Your unsent reports are visible to you only.
  • Reports you have sent to the council appear on the group's shared map and in its summaries, labelled "Team member" unless you choose to show your name. Admins can see who sent each one.
  • Services used to process a report: OpenStreetMap (street names), What3Words, Anthropic (suggesting a category from the photo) and Mapbox (the maps).

Your choices

You can download everything held about you from your account page, and ask an admin to delete your account and reports.
